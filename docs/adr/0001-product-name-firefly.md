# ADR 0001 — Product name: Firefly

## Status

Accepted on 2026-10-09. Working name; not a finalized brand.

## Context

The product's working name was "Yard Recovery." The opener video needed a name for its final card. Candidates: **Firefly** and **Relic**.

## Decision

Use **Firefly**. The opener ends on "FLOW." with "firefly" underneath, rather than spelling the name.

## Why

- Fireflies synchronize their flashes with no leader — a direct metaphor for agents and people coordinating.
- It gives the video a motif: a warm glow is the agent throughout.
- **Relic** was rejected because it connotes old/outdated, which is the exact pain the product removes (outdated site information).
- "flow" stays the payoff word because it names the outcome (work keeps moving); the name rides underneath.

## Consequences

- The product handoff (2026-10-10) still uses "Yard Recovery" and says naming isn't finalized. Firefly remains the working name; the repo and video should use one name consistently — decide at the event.
- "Firefly" collides with Adobe Firefly. Acceptable for a hackathon; revisit before any public launch.
- Brand treatment (logo, final palette) remains open.

## Revision (2026-10-11)

- **The video ends on the name, not "FLOW."** Muhammad: the product is Firefly and the name must stick with judges. New ending: FIREFLY with "the operating system for agents, robots and people" underneath ([ADR 0008](0008-firefly-is-an-os-for-mixed-workforces.md)).
- **"Firefly" is the name for the repo and the video.** "Yard Recovery" survives only as the name of the first workload.
- The firefly metaphor (synchronizing with no leader; the warm glow is the agent) stays available for the opener and the proposed attention lights. Visual identity is still undecided.
